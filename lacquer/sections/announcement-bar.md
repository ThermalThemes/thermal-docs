# Announcement bar

A promotional bar that sits above the header. Used for shipping cutoffs, drop dates, restock notes, sale messaging, or country-specific copy.

## What it does

Renders a single-line announcement at the top of every page. Optionally rotates through multiple messages on a timer. Optionally dismissible.

## Section settings

Documentation in progress. Settings include announcement messages, rotation timing, dismissible behavior, and color scheme.

## Blocks

- **Announcement** — Single message with optional link and country targeting.

## Example use cases

- **Shipping cutoff messaging.** "Ships today if ordered by 2pm PT."
- **Drop countdown.** "Next drop: Tuesday, 10am PT."
- **Free shipping threshold.** "Free shipping on US orders over $100."

## Notes

Keep messaging short. The announcement bar competes with the header for attention; long copy reads as noise.
