# Blog posts

Heading plus a grid of article cards pulled from a chosen blog. Used to surface drop notes, process journals, or studio updates on the homepage.

## What it does

Pulls articles from a single blog and renders them as a card grid with the heading area above. Each card shows the article's featured image, title, optional excerpt, and date.

## Section settings

Documentation in progress. Key settings: blog (blog picker), posts to show (range), columns desktop, columns mobile, show featured image, show date, show excerpt, show author, padding, color scheme.

## Blocks

- **Heading** — Section headline.
- **Subheading** — Small heading.
- **Description** — Body copy.
- **Button** — Optional "View all" or "Read the journal" routing.

## Example use cases

- **Latest drop notes** — Three articles from the Drops blog, used directly above the footer.
- **Studio journal** — Six articles from a single journal blog, used as a standalone editorial section.
- **Press coverage** — Six articles from a Press blog, used to surface external coverage.

## Notes

Article cards are configured at the theme-settings level (image aspect, excerpt length, hover behavior), not per-section.
