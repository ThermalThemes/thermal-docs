# Newsletter

Email capture form with heading, supporting copy, and a submit button. Used for drop announcements, restock notifications, and general list-building.

## What it does

Renders a heading, optional body copy, and an email input. Submissions post to Shopify's customer-form endpoint. Captures land in Shopify Admin → Customers, tagged as accepting marketing.

## Section settings

Documentation in progress. Key settings: width, content alignment, padding, color scheme.

## Blocks

- **Heading** — Section headline.
- **Subheading** — Small heading.
- **Text** — Body copy below the heading.
- **Email form** — The email input and submit button. Required.

## Example use cases

- **Drop list signup** — "Get the next drop in your inbox. No spam — just drop dates and edition notes."
- **Restock notifications** — Used on sold-out product pages.
- **Studio newsletter** — General-purpose signup, used in the footer or as a homepage closing section.

## Notes

Email captures go to Shopify Admin. To route them to a third-party ESP (Klaviyo, Mailchimp), connect the ESP to Shopify and it will sync new captures automatically.
