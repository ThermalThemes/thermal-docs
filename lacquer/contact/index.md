# Contact

## Get help with Lacquer

For setup questions, bug reports, customization help, or upgrade questions:

**Email:** [support@thermalthemes.com](mailto:support@thermalthemes.com)

**Contact form:** [thermalthemes.com/contact](https://thermalthemes.com/contact)

We respond within one business day, Monday through Friday.

## What to include

To get the fastest answer, include:

- Your **Shopify store URL** — the storefront where the issue occurs.
- Your **theme version** — visible in the theme editor at **Theme settings → Theme info**, or check the [Changelog](../changelog/).
- A **screenshot or screen recording** of the issue, especially for visual bugs.
- The **browser and operating system** you're using.
- The **steps to reproduce** — what you clicked, in what order, and what happened.

For setup questions, include the part of the theme you're configuring (which section, which template) and what you're trying to accomplish.

## Before you contact us

Many setup and customization questions are answered in:

- [Sections reference](../sections/) — every section's settings and behavior.
- [Customizing](../customizing/) — color schemes, typography, layout.
- [FAQ](../faq/) — the most common questions and answers.

If the question is still unanswered, contact us — happy to help.

## What we don't support

- **Custom theme modifications** — We don't write custom code for individual stores. For significant theme customization, work with a Shopify Expert or freelance developer.
- **Third-party app integrations** — For app-specific issues, contact the app's developer first. We can help with theme-side integration questions.
- **Shopify platform issues** — Issues with checkout, payments, shipping, or Shopify admin functionality are Shopify's domain, not the theme's. Contact [Shopify Support](https://help.shopify.com/en/support).
