# Lacquer

A graphic-led theme for drop-model brands — skateboard decks, apparel, audio gear, design objects. Lacquer treats production notes as content. Paper stocks, ink colors, edition sizes, photographer credits live on the product page, not in a marketing tab. The register is the buyer's, not the brand's.

## Who it's for

Independent labels selling small drops to buyers who follow the label across releases. Apparel brands cutting and sewing in small runs. Hard-goods makers — boards, hardware, instruments, tools — where the graphic and the spec both carry the work. DTC operators who'd rather show their materials than their lifestyle.

## What ships

- **7 homepage section presets** ready to drop into the theme editor
- **2 product page layouts** — gallery-left editorial and stacked-media drop register
- **14 templates** covering home, collection, product, cart, blog, article, search, page, 404, gift card, and four specialty layouts
- **33-section library** built on the thermal-base v1 substrate
- **Online Store 2.0** — section groups, JSON templates, app blocks
- **Lighthouse 99 / 100 / 96 / 100** on the production demo
- **Editor-grade settings depth** — no merchant ever opens a code editor
