# Getting started

This page takes you from installing Plinth to a store that looks like yours. It should take about ten minutes, plus the time you spend on product images.

## Install the theme

1. In your Shopify admin, go to **Online Store > Themes**.
2. Add Plinth from the Shopify Theme Store. It appears in your theme library as an unpublished theme, so your live store does not change yet.
3. Choose the preset you want when you add the theme: **Plinth**, **Lowfold** or **Ironwell**. [Presets](../presets/) describes each one. You can change every setting a preset makes later.
4. Click **Customize** to open the theme editor.

You can try Plinth for as long as you like before you buy it. You pay only when you publish.

## The first ten minutes

Work through these in order. Each one is in the theme editor.

### 1. Add your logo

Open **Theme settings** (the paintbrush or cog icon) and then **Brand**.

- **Logo**: your main logo.
- **Inverse logo**: a light version for dark backgrounds and for when the header sits over the opening section. If you leave it empty, the main logo is used everywhere.
- **Logo width**: 50 to 300 pixels.
- **Favicon**: the small icon in the browser tab. A 32 x 32px .png is recommended.

With no logo, the header shows your store name in the heading font.

### 2. Set up your menus

In the Shopify admin, go to **Content > Menus**.

- The header uses the menu called **Main menu** by default.
- The sign-off at the bottom of the page uses the menu called **Footer menu** by default.

You can pick different menus in the **Header** and **Sign-off** sections. See [Header, footer and sign-off](../sections/header-footer).

### 3. Build the opening section

On the home page, click the **Hero** section. It has three parts:

- **Headline**: two or three short lines. The last line stands on the Shelf.
- **Object**: choose a **Product**. Its first image stands beside or under the headline. For the best result use a cut-out image (a .png with a transparent background), 1600px tall or more.
- **Aside**: a short label, a line of text and a button.

See [Hero](../sections/signature#hero) for every option.

### 4. Point the home page at your collections

The home page sections arrive empty. Click each one and choose what it shows:

- **Product rail**: select the **Products** block and choose a **Collection**.
- **Collection mosaic**: choose a **Collection** for the flood tile and for each collection tile.
- **Tabbed collections**: choose a **Collection** for each tab.
- **Annotated product**: choose a **Product**.
- **Journal**: choose a **Blog**.

A section with nothing chosen shows a placeholder in the editor so you can see its shape.

### 5. Check a product page

Open any product in the editor (use the page selector at the top and choose **Products**). Check that the images, price and variant options look right. If your product images have a white or coloured background they are shown in a frame; if they have a transparent background they stand on the Shelf. See [The Shelf and product imagery](../shelf/).

### 6. Choose drawer or page for the cart

In **Theme settings > Cart**, set **Cart type** to **Drawer** (the cart slides in from the side) or **Page** (customers go to the cart page).

### 7. Add your social links

In **Theme settings > Social media**, paste the full address of each account you use. Leave the others empty. The links are shown by the Footer section's **Brand** block and on the password page.

## Before you publish

- Open the store on a phone, or use the phone preview in the editor.
- Add a product to the cart and go through to checkout.
- Check the collection page filters. Filters are managed in the Shopify **Search & Discovery** app, not in the theme.
- Read [Performance tips](../customizing/performance) if you have large images or several videos.

## Good to know

- **Each section's layout choice is usually on a block inside it.** Plinth's own sections call this the **Arrangement** or **Composition**. For example, to change a Product rail from Lead to Grid, click the **Products** block inside the section, not the section itself.
- **Plinth's own sections take their colours from one setting.** You do not pick a colour scheme for each one. **Theme settings > Ground and type > Ground** decides it for the whole store. See [Colour and grounds](../customizing/colour).
- **Duplicate the theme before large changes.** In **Online Store > Themes**, use **Duplicate** from the menu beside the theme. You then have a copy to go back to.
