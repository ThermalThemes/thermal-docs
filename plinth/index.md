# Plinth

Plinth is a Shopify theme built around one idea: **every product stands on one line.** That line is called the Shelf. It runs across the page, your products stand on it like objects in a gallery, and the name, variant and price read along it like a gallery label. The rest of the interface stays quiet, so the product and a single accent colour carry the page.

Plinth works best with product images that have a transparent background (cut-outs), because those are the images that can stand on the Shelf. Ordinary photographs work too; the theme frames them instead. [The Shelf and product imagery](./shelf/) explains exactly what happens with each kind of image and what sizes to upload.

## Who it suits

- Stores that sell physical objects with a clear shape: homeware, tools, furniture, lighting, ceramics, bags, shoes, electronics, bottles and jars.
- Small to medium catalogues where each product deserves room.
- Merchants who can supply, or are willing to make, clean product images on a transparent background.

## What you get

- **Three presets**: Plinth, Lowfold and Ironwell. Each sets the colours, fonts, spacing and home page arrangement. See [Presets](./presets/).
- **33 sections you can add to your pages**, plus the header, announcement bar, search overlay, sign-off, footer, cart drawer and back to top button. See [Sections](./sections/).
- **A product page built from blocks** you can reorder: gallery, badges, price, variant picker with swatches, quantity, buy buttons, inventory status, pickup availability, complementary products, assurances, description and collapsible rows.
- **Collection pages** with filtering, sorting, a choice of pagination, and promotional tiles inside the product grid.
- **A cart drawer or a cart page**, with a cart note and discounts shown.
- **Search** with suggestions as the customer types.
- **Translations** in English, German, Spanish, French and Italian, and support for right-to-left languages.
