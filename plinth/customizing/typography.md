# Typography

Plinth uses three fonts, each with one job.

| Setting | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| **Heading font** | The Hero headline, Statement sections, section titles, and the large word in the sign-off |
| **Body font** | Running text, buttons and form fields |
| **Accent font** | The label under each product, subheadings, figures, and other small labels |

All three are under **Theme settings > Typography** and use Shopify's font library, so they are served from Shopify's servers and need no extra setup.

## Type pairing

**Theme settings > Ground and type > Type pairing** adjusts the size, line spacing and letter spacing of large headings to suit a kind of font. It does not change the fonts themselves, so set both together.

| Type pairing | Choose a heading font that is | Example from the presets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Serif** | A serif at regular weight | Source Serif 4 heading, Geist body |
| **Grotesk** | A sans serif at bold weight | Geist bold heading, Source Serif 4 body |
| **Sans** | A sans serif at semibold weight | Geist semibold heading, Geist body |

If headings look too tight, too loose or too heavy after you change a font, try a different Type pairing.

## Text size

**Text size** (**Compact**, **Standard**, **Large**) scales text across the store. Standard suits most stores. Choose Large if your customers benefit from bigger text, or Compact for a dense catalogue.

## The label under each product

**Theme settings > Shelf > Label style** sets how the product label looks:

- **Mono capitals**: the accent font in capitals.
- **Mono sentence case**: the accent font as you typed the product name.
- **Body font**: the body font.

A monospace accent font, such as the presets' IBM Plex Mono, gives the gallery-label look and keeps prices aligned.

## Tips

- **Keep product names short.** The label is one line beside the price on larger screens.
- **Write the Hero headline as two or three short lines.** One line per row in the **Headline** field. The last line stands on the Shelf.
- **Use heading blocks at their named sizes.** A Heading block has an **Emphasis** option: **Quiet**, **Standard**, **Strong** or **Display**.
- **Check other languages.** German and French words are often longer. Preview your headlines in each language you sell in.
- **Choose fonts that include the characters your languages need.** If a font lacks a character, the browser substitutes another font for it.
