# Search

## Search overlay

Clicking the search icon in the header opens a search field across the top of the page. As the customer types, suggestions appear under it, grouped:

- Suggested searches
- Products
- Collections
- Pages
- Articles

Pressing Enter goes to the full results page.

The overlay is the **Search overlay** section in the header area.

- **Color scheme**.

If you remove the Search overlay section, the search icon goes straight to the search page instead.

What appears in suggestions, and which kinds of result are included, is managed by Shopify. The **Search & Discovery** app lets you add synonyms, boost products and hide products from search.

## Search results page

- **Color scheme**.

The results page has a search field, the number of results, and the results themselves, 24 to a page.

- **Products** are shown as cards on the Shelf, as on collection pages.
- **Pages and articles** are shown as titled links.
- **Filters and sorting** work as on collection pages, using the filters you set up for search in the Search & Discovery app.
- When nothing matches, a line says so and the search field stays ready for another try.

## Tips

- Add **synonyms** in Search & Discovery for words your customers use that are not in your product titles.
- Fill in product **types**, **vendors** and **tags**. Search reads them.
- If a product should not appear in search, hide it in Search & Discovery.
