# Support

## Get help with Mammoth

For setup questions, bug reports and questions before you buy, use the contact form:

**[thermalthemes.com/contact](https://thermalthemes.com/contact/)**

Say that your question is about Mammoth so your message reaches the right person.

We reply within 24 hours.

## What to include

To get the fastest answer, include:

- Your **store address**, for example `storename.myshopify.com`.
- The **page** where the problem appears. A link is best.
- What you **expected** to happen and what **happened** instead.
- The **steps** to see it: what you clicked, in what order.
- A **screenshot or screen recording**.
- The **device and browser** you were using, for example iPhone, Safari.
- Your **theme version**. In the theme editor, open **Theme settings**; the version is shown at the bottom.

For a setup question, say which section or setting you are working with and what you are trying to achieve.

## Before you contact us

- The [FAQ](../faq/) answers the most common questions.
- [The Shelf and product imagery](../shelf/) covers most image questions.
- If something looks wrong after installing an app, turn the app off briefly to see whether the problem goes away. If it does, the app's developer is the right person to ask.
- If you have edited the theme's code, check whether the problem also happens on a fresh, unedited copy of the theme.

## What support covers

Support covers Mammoth as supplied:

- Help with setup and with using the theme's settings and sections.
- Fixing defects in the theme.

Support does not cover:

- Changes to the theme's code, or problems caused by code changes.
- Custom design or development work.
- Problems caused by third-party apps.
- General Shopify questions, such as shipping, payments and taxes. [Shopify Support](https://help.shopify.com/) can help with those.

If you need custom work, a Shopify Partner can help. Before any code is changed, duplicate your theme so you have a copy to return to.

## Updates

Fixes and improvements are released as theme updates, free of charge, through the Shopify Theme Store. See the [Changelog](../changelog/).
