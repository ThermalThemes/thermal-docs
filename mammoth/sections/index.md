# Sections

Sections are the building blocks of each page. In the theme editor, click **Add section** to add one, drag to reorder, and click a section to see its options.

## How Mammoth's sections are organised

**The layout choice is on a block inside the section.** Most of Mammoth's own sections contain one fixed block that holds the main content and the layout choice, called **Arrangement** or **Composition**. For example, a Product rail contains a **Products** block; click it to choose the collection and whether the rail is a Lead, Drag or Grid arrangement. Fixed blocks cannot be removed or reordered.

**Most sections accept extra blocks.** Under the fixed blocks you can usually add **Heading**, **Subheading**, **Text**, **Button** and **Image** blocks, and blocks from apps you have installed.

**Colours come from the Ground setting.** Mammoth's own sections do not have a colour scheme option. See [Colour and grounds](../customizing/colour). The standard sections each have a **Color scheme** option.

**Spacing comes from one setting.** **Theme settings > Layout > Section spacing** sets the space between all sections.

## Shared blocks

These blocks can be added to most sections.

| Block | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Heading** | **Text**; **Emphasis**: Quiet, Standard, Strong or Display |
| **Subheading** | **Text**. A short line above a heading |
| **Text** | **Text**, with bold, italic, links and lists |
| **Button** | **Label** and **Link** |
| **Image** | **Image**; **Media ratio**: Natural, Square, Portrait, Landscape or Wide; **Link** |
| **Section header** | A fixed block at the top of many sections that holds its subheading, heading, text and button. **Alignment**: Start or Center |
| **Custom Liquid** | **Liquid code**. For an app snippet or your own code. Available where a section lists it |
