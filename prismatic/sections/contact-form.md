# Contact form

A contact form that delivers submissions to your store's customer email (set in Shopify admin under **Settings → Notifications**).

## Section settings

- **Content width** — `small`, `medium`, `large`.
- **Content alignment** — `left` or `center`.
- **Show phone number field** — Default off. **Show subject field** — Default on.
- **Padding top / bottom**, **Color scheme**.

## Blocks

Takes [theme blocks](./index#theme-blocks) — typically a Heading and a short Text block above the form.

## Notes

- The demo's contact page pairs this with a rich-text column carrying hours and turnaround expectations.
- Spam protection comes from Shopify's built-in hCaptcha on storefront forms.
