# Contact

## Get help with Prismatic

For setup questions, bug reports, customization help, or upgrade questions, use the contact form:

**[thermalthemes.com/contact](https://thermalthemes.com/contact/)**

We respond within one business day, Monday through Friday.

## What to include

To get the fastest answer, include:

- Your **Shopify store URL** — the storefront where the issue occurs.
- Your **theme version** — visible in the theme editor at **Theme settings → Theme info**, or check the [Changelog](../changelog/).
- A **screenshot or screen recording** of the issue, especially for visual bugs.
- The **browser and operating system** you're using.
- The **steps to reproduce** — what you clicked, in what order, and what happened.

For setup questions, include the part of the theme you're configuring (which section, which template) and what you're trying to accomplish.

## Before you contact us

Worth a quick check first:

- The [FAQ](../faq/) covers the most common setup and customization questions.
- The [Sections reference](../sections/) documents every section's settings and blocks.
- If a product or collection renders unexpectedly, preview it in the theme editor — most rendering questions trace to a setting or to missing content (images, variants, filters) rather than a defect.

## Scope

Support covers Prismatic as shipped — setup, configuration, and defects. Customization beyond theme settings (code edits, third-party app conflicts) is outside standard support, though we'll point you in the right direction where we can.
