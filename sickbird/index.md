# Sickbird

A loud, type-forward theme for graphic-led brands — skate and snow hard goods, screen-printed apparel, maker catalogs sold in small numbered runs. Sickbird is built around oversized display type used as structure, hard color-scheme inversions from section to section, and a single hot accent used like a trail marker. Dense and graphic, never sloppy.

## Who it's for

Board-sports brands and the labels around them — decks, boards, apparel, wax, hardware. Screen-print and maker operations where the graphic *is* the product. Streetwear and drop-model stores that want poster density instead of white space. Any store whose catalog photographs as graphics and atmosphere rather than studio-lit lifestyle.

## What ships

- **27 drop-in sections** plus configurable header, footer, and announcement bar — built on the thermal-base substrate
- **14 templates** — home, product, collection, collections list, cart, search, blog, article, page, contact, theme features, 404, password, gift card
- **4 color schemes** designed to alternate hard — paper, ink, reef green, safety orange
- **Display type scale** — most sections can render their heading at an oversized display size, up to poster scale
- **Collection filtering** with sidebar, top-bar, or off-canvas drawer layouts, removable filter chips, and load-more pagination
- **Mega menu** — three-level navigation with an optional feature image panel
- **Product gallery** with six desktop layouts, thumbnail rail, variant-image swap, and a contained zoom lightbox
- **Specification tables** that render from blocks or directly from a product metafield
- **Online Store 2.0** — JSON templates, section groups, theme blocks, app blocks
- **Lighthouse accessibility 97** on every core template, both viewports; performance averages 98 desktop / 88 mobile on the production demo
