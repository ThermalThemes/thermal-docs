# Article navigation

Previous / next article links, rendered from the current article's position in its blog. Belongs at the bottom of the article template.

## Section settings

- **Show article images** — The neighboring articles' featured images in the navigation. Default on.
- **Padding top / bottom**, **Color scheme**.

## Notes

- The section reads the article context; on templates without one it renders nothing. Add it to the article template only.
- With images off, navigation shows title and date alone — the quieter treatment.
