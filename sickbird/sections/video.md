# Video

A video section — Shopify-hosted upload or YouTube/Vimeo URL — with a cover image shown before play.

## Section settings

- **Video (uploaded)** — Shopify-hosted video. Takes precedence over the URL.
- **YouTube or Vimeo URL** — Used when no uploaded video is set.
- **Cover image** — Shown before external videos play; also renders as a static image when no video is configured.
- **Aspect ratio** — `16/9`, `21/9`, `4/3`, `1/1`.
- **Width** — `small`, `medium`, `large`, `full`.
- **Autoplay** — Mutes automatically when on. **Loop**.
- **Padding top / bottom**, **Color scheme**.

## Blocks

Takes [theme blocks](./index#theme-blocks) for content beside or above the video.

## Notes

- Always set a cover image — it's the section's mobile and pre-play face.
- Uploaded video gets Shopify's player and adaptive delivery; embeds load the provider's player on demand.
