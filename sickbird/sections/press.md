# Press

Press mentions with quotes — distinct from [Logo list](./logo-list), which shows logos alone. Each mention pairs a publication with a pulled quote and date.

## Section settings

- **Heading** — Section heading.
- **Display style** — `list`, `carousel` (default), or `grid`, with **Columns on desktop** (1–4) for grid.
- **Padding top / bottom**, **Color scheme**.

## Blocks

- **Press mention** — Publication logo (or **Publication name** text when no logo), quote, **Attribution date** (e.g. "December 2025"), link to the article.

## Notes

- The attribution date is worth filling in — dated quotes read as real coverage.
- `list` gives one large quote per publication; `grid` packs more mentions smaller.
