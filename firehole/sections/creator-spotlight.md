# Creator spotlight

A spotlight on a person behind the catalog: a maker, designer, guide, or athlete. Pairs an editorial portrait and bio with a rail of that creator's products, so the byline and the work sit together.

## What it does

Renders a portrait, name, role, bio, and optional signature alongside a product rail sourced from either a collection or a hand-picked product list. The layout puts the portrait left, right, or stacked above the rail, and an optional call-to-action links to the creator's full collection or page.

## Section settings

- **Portrait** — the creator's image.
- **Name** with **Name display size** (`none`/`sm`/`md`/`lg`/`xl`).
- **Role** and **Bio** — short attribution and description.
- **Signature** — an optional signature image.
- **Layout** — `portrait_left`, `portrait_right`, `stacked`.
- **Product source** — `collection` or `products` (hand-picked).
- **Collection** / **Products** — the source for the rail.
- **Rail count** — how many products to show.
- **CTA label / link / style** — `button` or `link`.
- **Padding top / bottom** — 0–160px.
- **Color scheme** — Section-level scheme.

## Example use cases

- A gear outfitter crediting the maker or guide behind a product line with a byline and their picks.
- A founder or designer feature paired with their signature pieces.
- A guest-collaboration drop with the collaborator front and center.
