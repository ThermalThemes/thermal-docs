# Bundle builder

A build-your-own-bundle section. Shoppers pick products from a chosen collection up to a defined pack size; the section tracks progress toward the minimum and surfaces the savings as the bundle fills.

## What it does

Shows products from a collection in a grid and lets the shopper add them to a bundle bounded by minimum and maximum item counts. Progress and savings messaging update as items are added, and added items are tagged with a line-item property so the bundle is identifiable in the cart and order.

## Section settings

- **Heading** with **Heading display size** (`none`/`sm`/`md`/`lg`/`xl`).
- **Collection** — the source of bundle-eligible products.
- **Products to show** — how many to display.
- **Min items / Max items** — the bundle's required and maximum size.
- **Columns (desktop)** and **Columns (mobile)** — `1` or `2` on mobile.
- **Progress text** — message shown as the bundle fills toward the minimum.
- **Savings text** — message surfacing the bundle discount.
- **CTA label** — the add-to-cart action label.
- **Pack property key / value** — the line-item property written onto bundled items.
- **Padding top / bottom** — 0–160px.
- **Color scheme** — Section-level scheme.

## Example use cases

- A "build a 3-pack" or "pick any 5" offer from a collection.
- A mix-and-match bundle with a volume discount.
- A starter set or curated kit assembled by the shopper.
