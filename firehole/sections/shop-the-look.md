# Shop the look

A lifestyle image with product hotspots, paired with an adjacent panel of the tagged products. Product-only by design — for annotated content popovers, use [Hot spots](./hot-spots).

## Section settings

- **Subheading / Heading** — Section header, with **Heading display size** (`none`–`xl`).
- **Image** — The lifestyle image.
- **Image aspect ratio** — `adapt`, `square`, `portrait`, `landscape`. Default portrait.
- **Image position on desktop** — `image_left` or `image_right`.
- **Padding top / bottom**, **Color scheme**.

## Blocks

- **Product hotspot** — Horizontal/vertical position (0–100) plus a product.

## Example use cases

- **The full kit.** One atmospheric rider photo, three or four hotspots — board, jacket, wax — with the product panel doing the selling beside it.

## Notes

- The photography carries this section. Atmospheric, mid-motion imagery in the brand register beats styled product flats.
- Keep hotspots to 3–5; beyond that the panel outgrows the image.
