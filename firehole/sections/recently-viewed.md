# Recently viewed

A grid of products the current visitor has viewed, populated from browser storage. Empty for first-time visitors — the section hides itself until there's something to show.

## Section settings

- **Products to show** — 2–12, default 6. **Columns** — desktop 2–6, mobile 1–2.
- **Enable carousel on mobile** — Default on.
- **Padding top / bottom**, **Color scheme**.

## Blocks

Takes [theme blocks](./index#theme-blocks) for the header area.

## Notes

- View history is per-browser (localStorage), not per-account — it doesn't follow customers across devices.
- Strong placements: the cart page (surfacing considered alternatives at the decision point) or the bottom of product pages.
