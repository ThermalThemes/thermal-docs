# Trust icons

A compact row of reassurance: shipping, returns, secure payment, warranty, support. Each item pairs a built-in icon with a short heading and line of text.

## What it does

Lays out icon items in a configurable grid. Each item picks from a built-in icon set and carries its own heading and text. Sizing and alignment are section-level; the icons inherit the section color scheme.

## Section settings

- **Columns (desktop)** and **Columns (mobile)** — `1` or `2` on mobile.
- **Icon size** — `small`, `medium`, `large`.
- **Alignment** — `left`, `center`.
- **Padding top / bottom** — 0–160px.
- **Color scheme** — Section-level scheme.

## Blocks

One block type: **Icon item**.

- **Icon** — `shield`, `returns`, `shipping`, `payment`, `secure`, `certified`, `support`, `package`, `eco`, `global`.
- **Heading** — short label.
- **Text** — supporting line.

## Example use cases

- A reassurance strip below the buy box or in the footer group.
- Shipping, returns, and warranty promises on the homepage.
- Sustainability or certification badges with a line of context each.
