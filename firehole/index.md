# Firehole

Firehole is Thermal Themes' outdoor theme — one theme, a complete demo store. It pairs editorial-landscape storytelling with the spec-and-materials merchandising technical gear needs, and ships a complete, fully merchandised demo store. Named for the Firehole River — where Yellowstone's hot springs feed the water, fire and water in one place. (Grand Prismatic Spring, which gave [Prismatic](/prismatic/) its name, drains into the Firehole; the two themes are the same place on the map.)

Firehole ships with the **Firehole** preset — a bright-but-refined register, hot saturated colour held under quiet type and layout — and a complete demo store, branded **Headwaters**, a Montana-rooted gear outfitter. The preset is a starting point you replace with your own content. See [Presets](./presets/) for how the preset is configured.

## Who it's for

Outdoor, gear, and apparel brands whose storefront has to read as credible *and* aspirational — real materials and field-tested specs under big, confident imagery. Outfitters, technical-apparel labels, and equipment makers that want editorial polish without losing the spec depth a buyer actually needs to decide. The bright, saturated palette is a brand axis, not a loud one: type and layout stay disciplined, the colour carries the energy.

## What ships

- **33 drop-in sections** plus configurable header, footer, and announcement bar — 52 section files in total — built on the thermal-base substrate
- **14 templates** — home, product, collection, collections list, cart, search, blog, article, page, contact, theme features, 404, password, gift card
- **Configurable colour schemes** designed to alternate per section — define as many as you need; the Firehole preset ships a bright high-country palette
- **Specification tables** that render from blocks or directly from a product metafield — capacity, load, weight, dimensions, materials, weather rating, fabric composition
- **Spec-on-card merchandising** — collection cards can carry one key spec (e.g. "40L · 1,100g") beside the title, a deliberate gear-store tell
- **Collection filtering** with sidebar, top-bar, or off-canvas drawer layouts — built to filter by department, activity, capacity, and material — with removable filter chips and load-more pagination
- **Product gallery** with six desktop layouts plus four mobile layouts, thumbnail rail, variant-image swap, and a contained zoom lightbox
- **Editorial depth** — shop-the-look, hot spots, creator spotlight, and a Field Journal blog for trip reports, gear guides, and materials stories
- **Display type scale** — most sections can render their heading at an oversized display size for big landscape moments
- **Mega menu** — multi-level navigation with an optional feature-image panel
- **Online Store 2.0** — JSON templates, section groups, theme blocks, app blocks
